The files in this directory summarize a five-case strict-protocol Medium experiment. Six one-factor beta/gamma settings were evaluated at seed 42, and five training seeds were evaluated at the reference setting. The results use held-out tests, Controlled R0 attribution and E5 name-level ground truth. `aggregate.py` recomputes summaries from saved scalar observations and writes `summary.json` and two readable tables; `validate.py` optionally compares them with saved values. They do not retrain the 50 evaluated models or recompute their raw attribution scores. Pairwise seed comparisons share models and are not independent experimental samples.
