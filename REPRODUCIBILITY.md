The supported executable task is archival statistical reproduction: the included scripts aggregate saved case-level measurements for Tables III–IX, recompute the recorded confidence intervals and validate reported table-body values. The C10 scripts also recompute parameter and seed-stability summaries from archived scalar records. These tasks do not run the original generators, neural training, CCI attribution or BSM closed-loop simulations.

The package contains reference implementations and source-checked generation-procedure notes, but not a validated full from-scratch historical reproduction workflow. The original sample-level split indices for historical Tables III, V and IX are unavailable; later strict-protocol partitions and BSM pairing-group records are supplied as separate records. Some historical run configurations and random-number states are incomplete. The two relative-EQI-reduction values (4.2% overall and 7.1% storm) in the note to Table VIII cannot currently be recomputed independently from the distributable archived denominators in this package; the tabulated body statistics and specified confidence intervals are checked by the scripts.

For the data-generation assumptions, including synthetic rule-generated expert actions and the distinctions among SEEK-Sim, controlled SCM and BSM, see `data_generation/DATA_GENERATION_PROCEDURE.md`.
